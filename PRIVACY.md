# Privacy

**Short version: your voice stays on your computer.**

- Vocalkeep has no account, no analytics, no tracking and no cloud. The recording app never
  connects to the internet.
- Recordings, the voice bank, legacy messages and finished voices are ordinary files in a folder
  you can open (Settings › Voice bank › Open folder). Deleting them deletes them.
- The voice builder downloads one thing: the open "starting voice" checkpoint you chose, from
  Hugging Face. It uploads nothing.
- Backups and exports are only written where you choose.

If you give your voice bank to someone to build your voice on their computer, it contains your
recordings: share it like you would share personal photos.

A voice built from your recordings sounds like you. Keep it as you would keep a key, and only
install it where you want it to speak for you.
