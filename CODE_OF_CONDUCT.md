# Code of conduct

Vocalkeep is built for people going through some of the hardest moments of their lives, and for
the families, friends and clinicians around them. Contributors are expected to bring the same
care to this project.

- Be kind, patient and specific. Assume good intent.
- Write for everyone: plain words, no jargon where a simple word works.
- Respect privacy. Never ask for, share or attach someone's recordings or medical details.
- Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not welcome in any form.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that don't follow this code. To report a
problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's security advisory form.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
